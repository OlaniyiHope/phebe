Frequently Asked Questions

How many universities should I apply to?

We generally recommend 4 to 6: a mix of ambitious, realistic, and safe options based on your grades and test scores. Applying to too many rarely improves your odds and adds unnecessary fees.

How long does the admission process take?

Most universities respond within 4 to 8 weeks of receiving a complete application, though this varies by intake and program. We track every file so you're never left guessing.

Do I need to pay application fees separately?

Yes, university application fees are set by the institution and paid directly to them. Our processing support itself carries no separate charge for most partner universities.

What if I don't meet a university's entry requirements yet?

We'll flag any gaps early — whether that's a test score, a missing prerequisite, or a document — and suggest foundation programs or alternative universities where you're a stronger fit.

Can I apply to universities in more than one country at once?

Yes. Many students keep options open across two or three countries. We'll manage each application's separate requirements and deadlines in parallel.

What happens after I receive my offer letter?

Once you accept and pay your deposit, we hand your file to our visa team, who use the same documents to start your study permit application without delay.
