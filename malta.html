Study in Malta

Discover world-class education in the heart of the Mediterranean

Malta has become one of Europe's fastest-growing destinations for international students seeking globally recognized qualifications in a safe, English-speaking environment. Located in the heart of the Mediterranean, Malta combines excellent education with a rich cultural heritage and a vibrant student lifestyle.

As a member of the European Union, Malta offers internationally accredited universities and colleges with programs taught entirely in English. Students benefit from modern learning facilities, multicultural campuses, affordable tuition fees, and excellent career opportunities after graduation.

Whether you're pursuing a Bachelor's degree, Master's degree, professional diploma, or English language program, Malta provides a high-quality educational experience while allowing students to enjoy beautiful coastlines, historical landmarks, and one of the safest environments in Europe.

At Phebe Consultancy, we guide students through every stage of their study-abroad journey—from selecting the right university and course to securing admission, obtaining a student visa, and preparing for life in Malta.

Universities and Courses Available

Why Study in Malta?

Malta's higher education system follows the European Higher Education Area (EHEA) framework, ensuring degrees are recognized across Europe and many countries worldwide. Institutions emphasize academic excellence, practical learning, research, and industry collaboration.

Higher Education in Malta

Foundation & Diploma Programs: Designed to prepare students for undergraduate study or professional careers.

Bachelor's Degrees: Undergraduate programs across business, science, engineering, health, information technology, arts, tourism, and more.

Postgraduate Degrees: Master's and Doctoral programs offering advanced research and internationally recognized qualifications.

Public Universities

Malta's public institutions provide internationally recognized degrees, affordable tuition, cutting-edge research facilities, and strong links with European universities.

Private Institutions

Private universities offer flexible study options, smaller class sizes, modern campuses, and programs designed to meet global industry needs.

International Learning

Students from more than 100 countries study in Malta, creating a diverse, multicultural environment where English is the primary language of instruction.
