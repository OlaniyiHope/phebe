Frequently Asked Questions

When should I apply for my student visa?

As soon as you have your offer letter and financial documents ready. We recommend starting at least 3 to 4 months before your intake to allow for processing time and any follow-up requests.

What's the most common reason visas get refused?

Insufficient or unclear proof of funds, and a study plan or letter of explanation that doesn't align with the applicant's academic and financial background.

Do I need to attend a visa interview?

It depends on the destination and visa category. Where an interview is required, we run mock sessions beforehand so you know what to expect.

Can you help if my visa was previously refused?

Yes. We review the refusal letter, identify the specific gap that was flagged, and rebuild your application to address it directly before reapplying.

How long does visa processing take?

Processing times vary by country and season, typically ranging from a few weeks to a few months. We track your file and give you realistic timeline estimates throughout.
