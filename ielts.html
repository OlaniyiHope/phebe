Frequently Asked Questions

Academic or General Training — which do I need?

Most university programs require IELTS Academic. General Training is typically for work or immigration purposes rather than degree study — we'll confirm which applies to you.

How long are IELTS results valid?

IELTS scores are typically valid for two years from the test date. We'll help you time your test so results stay valid through your intended intake.

What if I don't hit the required band score?

We help you book a retake and, where a program allows it, look into pathway or foundation programs that accept a lower band score with an English top-up course.

Can I take a computer-delivered test instead of paper-based?

Yes, where available at your test centre. Computer-delivered tests are usually accepted by universities and typically release results faster.
